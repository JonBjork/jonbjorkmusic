How It's Built

Deep Dives And Intensives.

Most of the technique material comes in two formats, and they're meant to be taken in order.

A Deep Dive

A 30-minute practice session a day for 30 days. It builds correct practice habits, fixes the problems you already have with a technique, and if the technique is new to you, it teaches you to play it correctly from the beginning. I highly recommend doing the Deep Dive before the Intensive for the same technique.

An Intensive

The full eight-week program for one technique. Every week you get a new 60-minute practice routine, so across the eight weeks you're never repeating the same hour twice.
