Common Questions.

How much time do I need each day?
Ten minutes. Each workout is a focused, self-contained ten-minute session. Do one a day, repeat your favorites, or string a few together when you have more time. The point is consistent, quality reps, not marathon sessions.
Do I need a certain skill level?
The workouts span a wide range, from foundational builders to advanced runs, so there's room to grow whether you're an advancing beginner or a seasoned player. You should be comfortable reading basic tab and have some picking under your fingers.
What formats do I get?
Every workout comes with a play-along video, a fully notated PDF tab, and MIDI and Guitar Pro files so you can slow things down and loop sections at any tempo.
