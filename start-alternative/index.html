My Story With This

How Practice Room Pro Came To Be.

Hi, I'm Jon, and my journey with serious guitar instruction began when I enrolled at the Guitar Institute of Technology in Los Angeles, studying with players like Brett Garsed and T.J. Helmerich. That was the first time I'd been around teachers who treated practice like a system, not a vibe.

Through 25 years of teaching full time, I learned that the players who actually break through aren't the ones who watch the most lessons. They're the ones who pick fewer things and stay with them long enough for the work to land. The skill always lives in time-on-task. Always.

It was inside lesson after lesson, watching smart and dedicated guitarists hit the same wall, that I realized the problem was almost never talent or hours. It was how the work was put together. Every student had a notebook full of half-started lessons. Almost none of them had a single course they'd finished.

This sparked a desire to build one place where the routines were already written, the app counted every minute, and the feedback loop was a day away instead of a year away. Where the question wasn't "what should I practice today" but "how did today's session go."

That is why I created Practice Room Pro, to give serious players the system I wish I'd had back when I was the one stuck.
