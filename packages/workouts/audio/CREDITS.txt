Upstream sources as identified by the collection:
Electric guitar: Karoryfer Samples — https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples
Nylon guitar: quartertone, classicalguitar-multisampled, Freesound pack 11573.

The MP3 recordings are bundled unchanged. Playback selects the nearest sampled
pitch and applies pitch shifting, a short release envelope, gain and low-pass
filtering. No endorsement by the original creators is implied.

The upstream README, sample-source-info and code license are retained here.

Grand piano and harpsichord: FluidR3 GM, Frank Wen and contributors,
pre-rendered and packaged by Benjamin Gleitzman / midi-js-soundfonts.
Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages/FluidR3_GM
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported

Selected MIDI notes 40–88 extracted from the MP3 data-URI bank without audio
re-encoding. Playback applies gain, filtering and instrument-specific releases.
