The Part That Builds Speed

Most Tools Stop At "Slow It Down." That's Where The Work Starts.

You'll probably use this more than every other feature combined.

Anybody can slow a video down. Building it back to full speed is where progress is won or lost, and that's where most players stall. They jump the tempo too soon, it falls apart, and they never find out how close they were.

The speed trainer does the climbing for you. Set a starting tempo and tell it how many reps you want at each level. Play your reps, and when you've earned it, the Looper bumps the tempo and keeps going. No stopwatch, no metronome math, no remembering where you left off yesterday. Clean repetitions and automatic progression until you reach full speed.

Picture a real session. You find a lick, set the loop, drop it to 50%, and play five clean reps. The Looper takes you to 55%, you play five more, then 60%, and it keeps climbing. Thirty minutes later you've actually practiced, instead of dragging a timeline around.

That's the difference between owning a passage and watching someone else play it.
