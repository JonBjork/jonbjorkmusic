The goal of this module is simple:

✅ Learn all seven 3-note-per-string shapes in the major scale (and its related modes)

✅ Clean up your alternate picking technique, from both hands.

I'll be demonstrating everything in the key of **C major**, but if you're already comfortable with that key, just pick a less familiar one. This way, players at any level can get the most out of this routine.

---

⏱️ **This routine is divided into 4 parts and takes about 25 to 30 minutes:**

1. **Six-string shapes**
2. **Two-string groups**
3. **Single-string sequences**
4. **Picking-hand focus exercises**

---

We'll be using **three core practice tools** throughout this routine:

🎯 *Transition Time*

🎯 *Finger Choreography*

🎯 *Planting*

Each tool will be fully explained in the video lesson for its section.

> 🔁 It's very important to follow the instructions closely, and if you do, this will be one of the most efficient ways to improve your technique and internalize these shapes for life.
