The Real Problem

You're Not Stuck
Because You're Lazy.

You're stuck because there's too much to practice, and nobody is telling you what to drop. There has never been more free guitar instruction out there. And somehow that has made it harder to get better, not easier.

01
Too Many Lessons. Too Many Voices.
Every channel teaches the same things in different ways. And every teacher sounds like they know what they're talking about. With no way to tell what's good from what isn't, every session starts with a choice you can't really make.
02
You Start Things You Never Finish.
A new video shows up and you switch to it. A new lick catches your ear and you switch again. Half-started lessons pile up. Nothing builds, because nothing got the time it needed to stick.
03
You Don't Practice Anything Enough.
Fifteen minutes here, fifteen minutes there, across five different things. That keeps things going. It doesn't make them better. If one technique is holding you back, it needs real time over weeks.
Choose The Bottleneck

One Clear Target
Beats Ten Random Tabs.

Practice Room Pro is built around focused blocks. Pick the thing that's actually holding you back. Work on it long enough for the skill to change. Then move to the next target with better hands and better ears.

01
Expose The Problem
Slow it down until you can hear exactly which transition, string change, or accent breaks first. The thing you've been working around for years is usually one specific motion, not the whole technique.
02
Train The Motion
Build the movement with transition-time practice, small loops, and tempo targets that don't let slop survive. This is the work Virtuoso Lab and Technique Lab are built for.
03
Make It Transfer
Move the mechanic into sequences, phrases, and real musical contexts so it stops being an exercise and starts being something you can play.

The Eight Labs

Eight Focused
Practice Modes.

Each Lab works on one skill the right way. No filler. No busywork.

01
Virtuoso Lab
Technique built on how your hands actually learn. Most of the improvement happens during the short rests between reps. Virtuoso Lab builds your work in small groups of clean reps with rest built in. Hit fifteen clean reps at tempo and the BPM goes up.
02
Technique Lab
Remove your weak spots through massive repetition. Pick a lifetime rep goal for the move you want to own, like ten thousand clean reps of an alternate picking shape. Chip away at it, session by session.
03
Systematic Chops Lab
Train every two-string picking combination, one at a time. Systematic Chops walks you through every combination on the neck, so you build complete coverage instead of comfort zones.
04
Rhythm Lab
Master every rhythmic subdivision. Work through one rhythm at a time so eighth-note triplets, sixteenths, and the ones in between actually feel different to you. Not math you're doing in your head.
05
Ear Lab
Learn to play what you hear. A melody plays in a key. You find it on the fretboard. The link between your ear and your hands gets stronger every session, until you stop guessing and start hearing the lines you want to play.
06
Harmony Lab
Hear harmony by playing it. Voice-led triads moving through real progressions, so chord changes stop sounding like separate grips and start sounding like music going somewhere.
07
Fretboard Lab
See scales and arpeggios across the entire neck. Stop running the same shape in the same position. Build a complete map of where every note lives and how every shape connects.
08
Target Tone Lab
Stop skating over the changes. Anchor in a pentatonic shape, pick a chord tone to target, and the Lab walks you through the progression. Your lines start outlining the harmony instead of running scales over it.

About Me

Jon Bjork

Practice Room Pro is the membership I wished I'd had when I was stuck.

I started teaching full-time in 2001 and have worked with students at every level ever since. I've also spent years composing orchestral music, nearly 700 tracks for Epidemic Sound, heard on channels like MrBeast and Hot Ones.

My goal is simple: make the path to better playing as clear and direct as possible for every single member.
