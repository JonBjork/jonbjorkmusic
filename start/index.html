Practice System.

I'm Jon Bjork. Guitarist, composer, and teacher for over 25 years. This site exists to fix one problem: intermediate guitarists who know plenty but have stopped getting better.

The Idea In 30 Seconds

You have access to more guitar instruction than any player in history. If information made people better, you'd be done by now.

The players who break through the plateau are the ones whose practice has structure: a plan, a schedule, tempo targets, and someone checking their work.

That's why everything on this site is built to make you actually practice, and to make that practice count.
