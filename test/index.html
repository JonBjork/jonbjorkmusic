The Truth

Guitar education has grown endless. There is no shortcut. No new tutorial replaces focused practice, honest feedback, and time.

Our Philosophy

Practice with structure you can sustain, on material worth the time, and measure the result. If your playing isn't changing, your practice changes.

Who This Is For

Intermediate players and up, from every walk of life, done with tutorial hopping. If you're a beginner, build the basics first. This room will be here when a pentatonic scale and tab feel comfortable.

Common Questions

Before You Join.

Am I locked into anything?

No. Cancel any time from your Skool profile. And canceling doesn't kick you out: you stay in the community as a free member.

Do I get personal replies from Jon?

Yes. Post a clip or a question any day and I respond with text or a quick video, whichever solves it fastest. The weekly live is on top of that, not instead of it.

Who is actually inside?

Committed intermediate and advanced players. Some are working through their first intensive, some have played for decades. What everyone shares is showing up and doing the work.

What happens when I join?

You start with an assessment: a short questionnaire plus a couple of clips of your playing. From that I build your practice plan. Then the rhythm is simple: run your routine, check in, and show up when you can.
