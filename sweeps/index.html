How to practise this

Play the pattern twice in each position. Move up a half step until your index finger reaches fret 17, then come back through 16, 15 and all the way to 1. Play position 17 once on the journey, with the same two repetitions.

Follow the picking strokes. Keep the motion relaxed, let each note sound clearly, and stop the previous string from ringing into the next. Lower the tempo if you need to.

Each exercise stops on its own. Take a moment, then start the next. If you pause midway through a position, resume repeats that position with a fresh count-in.
